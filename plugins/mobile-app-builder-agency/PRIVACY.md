# Privacy policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Mobile App Builder Agency is a native instruction and reference package. It has no plugin-owned backend, account system, database, telemetry SDK, automatic account access or declared network connection. Its readable helpers only print a plan or read a prepared local catalog when explicitly requested. Installing the agency does not start research or read credentials.

Claude and any tools the user explicitly connects process prompts and authorized project files under their own terms and access controls. Workflows can guide development in a consuming app, package installation, provider APIs, builds, analytics or store operations within the user's scope. Credentials and user data for that consuming app are separate from plugin installation; do not paste them into chat, source, public logs, screenshots or issue reports.

Research may use public sources and user-provided exports without a connected account. The optional Mobile App Builder Research integration is separately installed and has its own declared Apify endpoint, sensitive user configuration and privacy disclosure. Core does not read existing environment tokens, CLI stores or credential files to authenticate it.

User-directed research can collect app listings, reviews, ad examples, search results and professional creator profiles, including personal data in public sources. Keep raw data in the consuming user's controlled and ignored research folder, collect only what is necessary and permitted, minimize retention, and redact shared summaries. Provider/source terms and the AI host's own policies apply. No plugin-owned retention schedule or cloud research storage is supplied.

Installing a plugin or configuring an account does not authorize paid research, private data collection or outreach. The owner's selected source, scope, account and budget remain explicit. Report privacy or security concerns through the source repository's support and security process without disclosing credentials or personal data.
