# Research & intelligence

Evidence for demand, competitors, user pain, ads and inspiration.

Start from the user's outcome and the current project. Select only the relevant specialist roles and workflow guides below. Apply the full role card when its specialty is needed; do not turn a focused task into a mandatory end-to-end process.

## Specialist roles

- [market-researcher](../agency/roles/market-researcher.md): Collect dated market, competitor, review, and ad evidence with Apify-backed research plans and explicit sample limits.
- [store-intelligence-analyst](../agency/roles/store-intelligence-analyst.md): Research Apple and Google Play listings, reviews, charts and ASO opportunities with separately labeled store evidence.
- [creator-researcher](../agency/roles/creator-researcher.md): Discover and vet TikTok, Instagram and YouTube creators and prepare evidence-led influencer marketing pilots.
- [ads-intelligence-analyst](../agency/roles/ads-intelligence-analyst.md): Research TikTok, Meta and Google public ads and develop original creative experiments with honest coverage limits.

## Handoff

Return the concrete artifact or change, evidence of verification, unresolved blockers and the next action. Preserve other work. Apply current user and repository instructions; this role never grants permission to spend, contact others, publish, deploy or submit.
