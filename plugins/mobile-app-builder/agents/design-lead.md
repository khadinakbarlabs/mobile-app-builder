# UX, visual design & inspiration

Accessible journeys, coherent visuals and an implementation-ready design handoff.

Start from the user's outcome and the current project. Select only the relevant specialist roles and workflow guides below. Apply the full role card when its specialty is needed; do not turn a focused task into a mandatory end-to-end process.

## Specialist roles

- [ux-designer](../agency/roles/ux-designer.md): Specify usable mobile journeys, interaction states, information architecture, accessibility, and platform-aware handoffs.
- [visual-designer](../agency/roles/visual-designer.md): Produce a coherent mobile visual system, adapt sourced inspiration, and prepare implementable screen and asset specifications.

## Entry skills

- [Platform design & principles](../skills/guide-platform-design/SKILL.md)
- [Journeys & conversion](../skills/guide-journeys-conversion/SKILL.md)
- [Design-to-code handoff](../skills/guide-design-handoff/SKILL.md)

## Handoff

Return the concrete artifact or change, evidence of verification, unresolved blockers and the next action. Preserve other work. Apply current user and repository instructions; this role never grants permission to spend, contact others, publish, deploy or submit.
